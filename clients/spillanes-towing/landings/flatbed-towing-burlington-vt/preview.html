When You Need a Flatbed

Some vehicles shouldn't be towed with wheels on the road.

AWD and 4WD vehicles

Towing an all-wheel-drive car on two wheels can damage the drivetrain. A flatbed carries all four off the ground.

Low-clearance and luxury cars

Sports cars and lowered vehicles are loaded carefully so nothing scrapes.

Damaged or non-rolling vehicles

Cars after an accident or with locked wheels go on the bed instead of being dragged.
