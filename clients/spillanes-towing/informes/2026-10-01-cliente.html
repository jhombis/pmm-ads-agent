FINDINGSWhat we found

  • Budget is going to the wrong searchesAbout 72% of spend went to "roadside assistance", which mixes in people looking for AAA, Geico, State Farm and similar memberships. Searches like "towing near me" and "tow truck near me" bring in better leads for less money.
  • We were paying for your own namePeople who search "Spillane's towing" already find you at the top of Google for free. Many of them are looking for a car that was towed, not for a new tow. We'll stop paying for those clicks.
  • The landing page needs a few fixesIt should say "24/7 Towing in Burlington" at the top, show a short 3-field form without scrolling, and have a thank-you page so we can count form leads correctly.
  • Google reviews are holding you backYour Google rating is 3.2 stars from 213 reviews. Drivers compare stars before they call, and other local tow companies show 4.5 or more. Ads can't fix this, but a steady flow of new reviews can.
  • Your fleet is your strongest selling pointOne wrecker, 16 flatbeds and 4 service trucks is something no competitor can claim, especially the national call centers that resell your area's calls. We'll put it front and center in the ads and on the page.
