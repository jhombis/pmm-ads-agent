The decision in five lines

  • 1 new Search campaign ("Search - Precision Towing KRV") replaces ENHPRM Radius. At $27/day the fragmentation limit is one campaign with 3–5 ad groups; brand is an ad group, not a campaign.
  • 4 ad groups: Tow Truck & Towing KRV (cities inside it via keyword insertion), Roadside Assistance, RV & Trailer Towing and Brand. 87 keywords on phrase and exact, zero broad.
  • Max. clicks with a $9 CPC cap while the new structure gathers data; Max. conversions after 15+ clean conv/month for 4 weeks; tCPA after 30 in 30 days. Presence in the real radius, Mon–Sun 6:30–22:30 account time.
  • The ceiling at $825 is ~27 calls/month if CPC is held at $9; ~10 if Max. conversions is left running at $24. CPC decides the outcome, not the structure.
  • No PMax, Display, broad or "24 hour". LSA on a parallel track if the client confirms CHP license and insurance.

Diagnosis

  • Bidding: Search CPC $24.14 from Sep 22 to Oct 5 against $7–8 in the three previous weeks and a niche maximum of $12.23. The break starts Sep 21/23 (clicks at $44–58) and never recovers: uncapped Max. conv. with IS 35%.
  • Waste: 30% of trackable spend went to competitor brands ($143.88; b&d towing and b&m towing are positive keywords) and 15% to shop repairs ($72.40, 0 conv: "starter and alternator repair near me" $65.42 in 2 clicks).
  • Pacing and schedule: $32/day (monthly cap $973) above the agreed $27; no ad schedule although the client answers 7–22. "lake isabella towing" on broad spent $136 with 0 conv while "kernville towing" converts at $21.
  • Tracking: no action dropped to zero (no tracking break), but "Form Fill" appeared (1 conv) with no thank-you page or redirect: verify what fires it before counting it. The own brand is negated and at the same time converts as a search term.
  • What to do: 1) Oct 7: budget $27, 14 keywords out, 408 negatives, broad to phrase; 2) read the time zone, schedule 6:30–22:30 and verify "Form Fill"; 3) new structure paused with Max. clicks capped at $9, activate Oct 20.
