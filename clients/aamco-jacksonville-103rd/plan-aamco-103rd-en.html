The 5 decisions in this plan

  1. Measurement first. Today 3 call sources count as primary at the same time. Nothing launches until that's fixed.
  2. 2 Search campaigns: BRAND 103rd ($8/day, impression share) and NON-BRAND 103rd ($60/day, Max Conversions, 5 ad groups).
  3. Phrase and exact, no broad. Transmission, the high-ticket service, gets priority; "oil change near me" stops being the catch-all.
  4. The current campaign and PMax 103rd are paused the same day the new ones go live.
  5. tCPA only on clean data (≥30 in 30 days). PMax, Display and broad match stay out for now.

Findings

  • We don't know the real cost per lead. The conversion rate jumped from ~17% to ~40% in April 2026, when CallRail and "Calls from ads" were added next to "Phone Call". That's a measurement change, not a performance change.
  • The campaign lives on brand searches, at ~$4.40 per click. Many of those calls are existing customers asking about their car.
  • Flat structure: 1 ad group, ~60 keywords (98% of spend on broad), 1 RSA rated "POOR" and 1 call-only ad.
  • Transmission gets only 15% of visible spend, even though it's the high-ticket service.
  • PMax 103rd ($16/day, tCPA $23) competes for the same calls; 63 of its 88 conversions are "Calls from ads".
  • Landing page: no thank-you page, and the subdomain form had 0 submissions in 90 days. All traffic lands on the generic home page.

Risks and competition

What could go wrong

Plan risks

  • No CallRail access, no launch. The new structure would be measured on the same inflated signal.
  • Reported CPA will go up once measurement is cleaned. The client needs to hear that first.
  • Less volume without broad match. If non-brand clicks fall more than 40% by day 14, we reopen generic terms in phrase match.
  • Budget < 3× CPL/day: learning will be slow, hence the buffer on the tCPA date.
  • Brand cannibalization: 4 locations bid on [aamco] (~$1,540 in 90 days, ~$5 per click). This gets solved at the account level.
  • The franchise controls the website: if it can't be edited, the thank-you page falls back to event-based tracking.
  • Spend nearly stopped in Feb–Mar 2026 (cause pending). Confirm the ad pool budget before tax-refund season.

Competition that matters

  • Duval County Transmission, ~1 mile away on the same street (8333 103rd St): NAPA 36/36 warranty, AAA with a 10% discount, open Saturdays.
  • Budget Automotive, 8670 103rd St: transmissions, family-owned since 1994.
  • Reputation: Everything Automotive (4.9★, 1,004 reviews) and JJ's (4.9★, 412) vs. AAMCO 103rd at 4.4★ with 507.
  • Chains (Firestone, Midas, Meineke, Take 5…) drive up the cost of generic and oil-change terms.
  • Opportunity: nobody on 103rd St advertises financing, and the current ad uses neither the AAMCO nationwide warranty nor AAMCO financing.
