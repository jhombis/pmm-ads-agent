The decision in five lines

  • Keep the campaign (PLL name) but change everything inside it: measurement first, negatives before touching the structure.
  • 3 ad groups: Towing (near me + wrecker + 24 h + cities), Exotic & Long-Distance (the existing, empty AG2) and Roadside on exact match only. That is the fragmentation limit for $825.
  • Maximize Clicks with a $6.50 CPC cap, phrase and exact, zero broad. Presence in 40 mi re-centered on Fredericksburg, 24/7. 1 RSA per group.
  • The ceiling at $825 is ~14–24 calls/month in month 2, not 35–55. Max. Conversions at 15 verified/month (~Nov); tCPA around Jan 2027.
  • No PMax, Display, remarketing or brand campaign. LSA waits for the Business Profile.

Diagnosis

  • Measurement: conversions are recording, but they don't prove leads. 3 of 6 came from "comal towing", "mission towing" and "junk cars". There were 8 calls for 4 Calls from Ads, and Form Fill fires without a thank-you page.
  • Volume: 53 clicks barely clear the 50-click threshold. The 11.3% CVR is indicative; nothing by hour, day or city is conclusive.
  • Traffic: 45.8% of what's visible is clear waste. Broad "roadside assistance" brings "car jumper", "toyotacare", "mopar" and "interstate battery".
  • Bid/CPC: $10.00, 2× mature accounts and +32% over the new-account cohort. In October 39% of IS is lost to budget: the money runs out on bad traffic. It needs filters, not more money.
  • What to do: 1) review the 8 calls with the client; 2) apply the negatives; 3) phrase and exact, $6.50 cap and 1 RSA per group.

        At ~126 clicks/month and ≈ 0 organic, the audience never reaches 1,000 users. And towing is an emergency purchase.
