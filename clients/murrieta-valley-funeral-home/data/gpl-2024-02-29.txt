                                              Packaged Services

Packaged service pricing is designed to ease the decision-making process.
The majority of families we serve select their service from the packaged service pricing.
The packaged prices are greatly discounted, and we cannot offer credit for deletions. Additions are
acceptable. Services over a 30 mile radius will incur an additional mileage charge.
All packages are priced at normal business hours.

Service packages do not include: Merchandise, cash advance items, sales tax, limousines, or motorcade
escort, unless stated in the package.

Pre-Need service plans are not eligible for Packaged Services.

In some cases, the volume of flowers may exceed the capacity of our primary vehicle for transport to the
cemetery or church. As a result, families may choose to transport the excess flowers separately or take them
home after the service. We offer the option to add a utility van for an additional charge. This van can transport
the surplus flowers to the cemetery and/or church, ensuring that all floral arrangements are handled with care
and respect. Please note that most cemeteries have specific regulations regarding the use of glass container.
