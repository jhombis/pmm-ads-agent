Account status today

The current campaign (live since Sep 1, spending since Sep 11) bids on Maximize Clicks with broad match, has a single RSA, sends everything to the homepage and carries 736 negatives inherited from another account. It is limited by budget (46.5% impression share lost) and CPC fell from $18 to $9.80 last week. In the visible search terms, 75% of spend had no commercial intent: it needs filters, not more money.

The decision in five lines

  • 1 new Search campaign ("Search NWA") replaces the current one; 3 weeks and 1 conversion leave nothing worth keeping.
  • 4 ad groups: Electrician - NWA (near me + every city) and Repair in P1; Panels and EV wait for their landing pages. Few on purpose: at ~80 clicks/month, more groups only fragment the data.
  • Maximize Clicks with a $12 CPC cap until 15+ conv/month; then Maximize Conversions. 1 RSA per group, phrase + exact, zero broad. Presence in Benton and Washington counties + Huntsville, Mon–Sun 5 AM–4 PM.
  • At $825 the ceiling is ~8–13 leads/month. Maximize Conversions and tCPA depend on more ad spend; the decision is made with 30 days of data (~Nov 1–15).
  • No PMax, Display, remarketing or brand campaign for now. LSA on hold.

      Client to-dos

      What we need from Pro Phase. The first three block the launch.

        Ready-to-send message

        To send to the client by email or text.

        Why not (yet)

        Performance Max

        Needs ~$150/day, a stable tCPA for 4 weeks, 30+ conv./month, video and modern spam protection. None are met.

        Broad match

        Requires a mature tCPA and reliable tracking. The current broad campaign showed the cost: 75% of spend without intent.

        Display / Demand Gen

        Local service with no audiences or budget; cheap, low-intent clicks.

        Brand campaign

        ~80 searches/month and organic at position 3. Opens at $2–3/day only if competitors bid on the brand.

        Remarketing

        ~80 clicks/month: the list won't reach 1,000 users.

        Emergency / 24h

        The client isn't 24/7. "emergency", "24 hour" and "after hours" are negatives.

        Risks & assumptions

        • Without ticket and margin there's no max CPL: $85 per lead is healthy if a panel job nets $500+, not if the typical job is a $150 outlet.
        • A conversion is a call ≥60 s, not a job. Monthly client sheet to mark which calls became jobs.
        • Budget-limited by design: ~2,000+ commercial searches/month and $825 buys ~80 clicks. Impression share lost to budget will be high.
        • No Business Profile access: no location asset and no Maps presence through Ads.
        • Mobile speed not measured; a score <40 becomes a blocker.
